Our story

Built for local makers and the people next door.

Vendly is a marketplace where homemade goods, food, and crafts find their way to the neighbours who’ll love them most.

We started Vendly because buying local shouldn’t be hard. Some of the best bread, jam, candles, and knitted goods are being made two streets away — but there was no simple way to find them.

So we built one. A marketplace that puts local makers first, with zero listing fees, simple tools, and a focus on the towns and neighbourhoods people actually live in. Whether you bake on weekends or run a full-time workshop, Vendly is your shopfront.

What we believe

Four principles that guide everything we build.

🏘️

Local first

Every search prioritises makers in your area — so pickup is easy and money stays in the community.

🤲

Fair for makers

No listing fees, transparent commissions, and tools that respect your time and craft.

🌱

Small is beautiful

We champion the kitchen-table businesses and weekend workshops as much as the big shops.

🔒

Safe & simple

Clear product pages, honest descriptions, and direct communication between buyer and maker.

💬

Human support

Real people answer your questions — whether you’re shopping or just getting started selling.

✨

Made with care

We’d rather have 100 great listings than 10,000 generic ones. Quality over quantity, always.
